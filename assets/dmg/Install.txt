INSTALL PASSPORT FILIGRANE

For macOS 14 or newer, on Apple Silicon and Intel.

1. Drag Passport Filigrane into the Applications folder beside it.
2. Wait for copying to finish, then eject Passport Filigrane in Finder.
3. Open Applications and double-click Passport Filigrane.

FIRST LAUNCH

This free release is ad-hoc signed, not signed with an Apple Developer ID
or notarized by Apple. macOS can block it on first launch because it cannot
verify the developer. Only approve a download you trust.

If macOS blocks it for this reason, dismiss the alert, open System Settings
> Privacy & Security, and find the message about Passport Filigrane.
Choose Open Anyway, confirm Open, and authenticate if requested.
The approval button is available for about an hour after the attempted launch.

If Open Anyway is missing, try opening the installed app again and check
Privacy & Security promptly. A work or school Mac may restrict this option;
ask its administrator. A warning that the app is damaged or will harm your
computer is a different condition: stop and consult the release page or
Apple's guidance. Do not disable macOS security protections.

Apple guidance: https://support.apple.com/en-au/102445

USING THE APP

Add an image or PDF, enter your watermark text, then choose Export all.
Your originals stay unchanged and processing stays on your Mac.

If Applications asks to replace an existing copy, cancel and keep your
current installation until you are ready to replace it with the new version.
